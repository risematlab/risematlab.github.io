RESEARCH · 2025-08-22

Natural hazards and bendable concrete

Buildings of the future may not need to break under natural hazards. 🌍🏗️

Excited to share our new publication in Materials and Structures: “Seismic and wind resistance of natural pozzolan-based engineered cementitious composites (NP-ECC): material characterization and numerical analysis.”

This study, led by my PhD students Diego Aparicio Vallejos and Matias Leon-Miquel, comprehensively demonstrates how buildings made of ultra-ductile concrete can withstand seismic and wind loading, a step toward resilient infrastructure.

Grateful for an amazing collaboration between our RISE Materials Lab with Universidad de los Andes (Álvaro Paul, Juan Silva‑Retamal), Purdue (Chengcheng Tao, Junyi Duan), and FAMU-FSU (Qian Zhang).

📄 Full paper access: https://rdcu.be/eBOLU
