Undergraduate researchers at Design Day

Another proud moment today for our RISE Materials Lab! 🟢⚪️

It was an absolute joy to see two of our outstanding undergraduate researchers, Nathan Denning and Kelly Couvreur, EIT, presenting their Capstone Project at the Michigan State University College of Engineering Design Day.

Both have been great assets to our team: Nathan has worked with us since his freshman year, and Kelly has been an integral part of the lab for the past two years. They have contributed tremendously to our major research initiatives, including Dr. Xiaoqiang Ni's self-heating, self-healing ductile concrete project and Matias Leon-Miquel's extraterrestrial bendable "MoonCrete" and "MarsCrete" project.

Their futures are incredibly bright: Nathan is heading to Stanford University to pursue his graduate studies (he is the 2nd undergraduate student from our group to Stanford) and Kelly will be staying right here at Michigan State University to continue her journey in graduate school.

Thank you both for your hard work and dedication to our lab and wish you the very best of luck as you embark on these exciting next chapters in your careers! 🚀
