Summer updates and new lab members

As the summer semester comes to a close, exciting updates are happening at the RISE Materials Lab at Michigan State University Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering! 🌞

This week, we welcomed our newest member, Dr. Peizhi Wang, joining us from FAMU-FSU College of Engineering as a postdoctoral scholar. He will focus on developing ultra-flexible concrete for extreme environments. To help him settle in, we held our group end-of-summer celebration at the Graduate Rooftop, enjoying the beautiful panoramic view of the Michigan State University campus. Welcome to the group, Peizhi!

Our new PhD student, Minseok Nam, has done fantastic research this summer, with a paper submission expected soon.

And two senior PhD students, Diego Aparicio Vallejos and Yinjian “Klay” Li, celebrated their front cover publication in ACS Omega — as a gift, I printed the cover as posters for them. Kudos to Klay and Diego!

Onward and upward to a great fall semester!  🚀
