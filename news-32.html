Celebrating RISE Ph.D. graduates

A full-circle moment for our RISE Materials Lab. 🎓

Last week, I had the incredible honor of hooding my first two PhD students, Xiaoqiang Ni and Matias Leon-Miquel. Standing on the stage at Michigan State University Breslin Center, I couldn’t help but look back to my own hooding ceremony couple of years ago with my advisor Kimberly Kurtis. Transitioning from a student receiving the hood to a mentor bestowing it is a powerful realization of academic cycle:

Mentorship isn't just about guiding a thesis. It’s about passing the torch to the next generation of researchers who will go further than we ever did.

Antonio and Matias have set an incredible standard for our group. It has been a privilege to watch you both grow from students into the leaders you are today. Congratulations again 🎊! I cannot wait to see your next endeavors. 🚀
