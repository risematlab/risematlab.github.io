Welcoming Minseok Nam

Exciting updates from our RISE Materials Lab! ✨

As kicking off summer 2026, we are excited to welcome our newest PhD student, Minseok Nam, to Michigan State University.

Coming to us from Korea Maritime & Ocean University, Minseok will be driving new research initiatives in advanced multifunctional cementitious composites, waste valorization, and multi-scale materials characterization.

It has been wonderful helping him get settled on our beautiful campus this past week. Welcome to the group, Minseok, and I am looking forward to a wonderful PhD journey together! 🚀
