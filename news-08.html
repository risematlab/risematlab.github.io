MSU spotlights Matias Leon-Miquel’s doctoral research

On February 22, 2024, the MSU Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering featured RISE doctoral student Matias Leon-Miquel in its student spotlight.

The feature traced his path from civil engineering studies in Chile to research at Michigan State University on cementitious composites for extreme environments. It also highlighted his published research on Chilean natural pozzolan.
