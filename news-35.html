AWARDS · 2026-01-22

Dr. Jin selected for ARPA-E IGNIITE

I’m thrilled to share that I’ve been selected by Advanced Research Projects Agency-Energy (ARPA-E) for the 2025 Inspiring Generations of New Innovators to Impact Technologies in Energy (IGNIITE 2025) program!

IGNIITE empowers early-career energy innovators whose work can turn disruptive and unconventional ideas into transformative technologies. I’m deeply grateful to ARPA-E for this support, which will ignite the next chapter of my career and accelerate our development on transformative construction materials for critical energy infrastructure.

This achievement would not have been possible without the dedication, creativity, and hard work of my group members at the RISE Materials Lab, as well as the continued support of my family, mentors, colleagues, and friends.

To learn more about my project and the other 2025 IGNIITE selectees, see official announcement here: https://lnkd.in/g2iyawzd
