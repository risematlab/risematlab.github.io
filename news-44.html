Postdoctoral opportunities at RISE

RISE LAB

Join our research team.

We’re hiring 1–2 Postdoctoral Scholars at RISE Materials Lab, Michigan State University. Our group designs advanced materials for energy infrastructure and resource resilience, and continues to expand that portfolio. If you like problems that sit between materials, chemistry, and process engineering, this is for you.

🔬 Who we’re looking for: PhD in civil, environmental, or chemical engineering, materials science, or a closely related field. Experience with materials characterization, thermodynamic modeling, LCA and TEA, or ML-guided materials design is a plus, not a requirement.

✨ Fully funded, full-time positions in a collaborative, vibrant group with hands-on mentoring and room to shape your own research direction.

⏱ Candidates who can start by early 2027 are especially encouraged to apply; applicants already in the U.S. may be able to start sooner. All qualified candidates are welcome.

📩 How to apply: submit a cover letter, CV, and contact information for three references through the MSU careers portal (link below, posting #1178681).

Please share with anyone who’d be a fit.
