RISE team opened recruitment for postdoctoral scholars

RISE opened recruitment for full-time postdoctoral research in sustainable infrastructure materials.

Recruitment details

On November 2, 2026, the RISE team opened recruitment for postdoctoral scholars in sustainable infrastructure materials at Michigan State University. MSU’s Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering advertised a full-time, one-year appointment with the possibility of renewal based on research productivity and continued funding.

The position required a Ph.D. in civil, environmental or chemical engineering, materials science or a closely related field. The posting highlighted materials characterization and thermodynamic modeling as desired experience. The lab’s recruitment announcement encouraged candidates available to start by early 2027.

The official MSU postdoctoral posting (#1178681) asked applicants for a cover letter, CV, and names and contact information for three professional references. The announcement set November 12, 2026, as the full-consideration date and stated that review would continue from that date until the position was filled.
