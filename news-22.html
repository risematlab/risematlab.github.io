Phase change materials offered a way to manage heat in concrete

Dr. Xiaoqiang Ni led a review of phase change materials (PCMs) in concrete with Wenyu Liao, Hongyan Ma, Álvaro Paul and Qingxu Jin. Their review in the Journal of Materials in Civil Engineering appeared online on October 11, 2026. These materials absorbed heat as they melted and released it as they solidified.

The review compared direct mixing, microencapsulation and porous carriers. Incorporation methods changed the balance between heat storage and mechanical performance. In pavements, PCMs could also limit temperature differences within slabs and reduce the risk of curling and thermal cracking.

The authors highlighted numerical modeling as a way to evaluate dosage and placement before testing. The review outlined where field studies could extend this work, including leakage, performance under repeated heating and cooling, and long-term durability. In a separate project, the lab installed bendable concrete for field testing at MSU.
