OUR MISSION

Materials that meet complex challenges.

The RISE Infrastructure Materials Laboratory at Michigan State University investigates advanced construction materials for infrastructure exposed to demanding loads, environments and multiple hazards.

01

Research across scales

We connect material composition and microstructure to performance in components and infrastructure systems.

02

An interdisciplinary approach

Our work brings together experimentation, materials characterization, mechanics, modeling and data-driven methods.

03

People and learning

Research and education bring students and collaborators together around practical engineering challenges.

TEACHING

Learning through engineering.

At Michigan State University, Dr. Jin teaches CE 406: Design of Concrete Structures, covering the design of reinforced concrete beams, slabs, columns and footings. He has also taught Materials Science of Concrete at Georgia Tech and contributed to instruction in infrastructure sustainability and materials technology.

LABORATORY

From characterization to testing.

RISE uses experimental and analytical methods to study cementitious materials and their performance. Research spans material characterization, mechanical testing and the evaluation of infrastructure applications.
