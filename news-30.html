AWARDS · 2026-06-15

ARPA-E IGNIITE award ceremony

Concrete that bends. Withstands extreme heat and cold. Heals itself.🔥Now imagine a future where America's geothermal wells, pipelines, and power plants are built on this concrete.

Last week, I was honored to receive the ARPA-E #IGNIITE Award for our work on "Transformative Ultra-Flexible Concrete (UFC) for Extreme Environments".🏆

The highlight of the week wasn't just the ceremony, it was demonstrating our UFC sample to both ARPA-E Director Conner Prochaska and NAE President Tsu-Jae Liu. Watching their reactions firsthand — that's when the research gets real.

Beyond the ceremony, the IGNIITE summer session was equally impactful, diving deep into T2M (technology-to-market) strategies and energy commercialization alongside program directors Laurent Pilon, Cory Phillips, Charlie Werth, Assel Aitkaliyeva, and James Seaba, and an incredible cohort of fellow awardees. The conversations pushed my thinking well beyond the lab.💡

Grateful to my team at Michigan State University, and Advanced Research Projects Agency-Energy (ARPA-E) for the recognition and the push to go further. And special thanks to Ray Duthu and ARPA-E Comms for organizing this exceptional week! 🙏
