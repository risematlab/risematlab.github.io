LAB NEWS · 2025-10-10

Field demonstration on the MSU campus

Four concrete slabs using different mixtures were installed to begin real-world testing of self-heating and self-healing bendable concrete.

SUMMARY OF THE MSU TODAY REPORT

On October 10, 2025, MSU marked the installation of four experimental concrete slabs along Engineering Research Court. Each uses a different mixture so the researchers can compare their performance during the winter.

Developed by Dr. Qingxu “Bill” Jin and Xiaoqiang “Antonio” Ni, the material combines flexibility and self-healing with the ability to store solar heat and release it near freezing. The team will monitor durability under foot traffic and its ability to melt snow and ice.
