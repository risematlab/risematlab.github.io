Xiaoqiang Ni defended his dissertation on self-healing concrete

Xiaoqiang “Antonio” Ni defended his dissertation, Self-Heating and Self-Healing Ductile Concrete, on April 22, 2026.

His research explored how ductile concrete could reduce the need for deicing salts and extend the service life of infrastructure in cold regions. His work covered materials design, characterization, scale-up and economic analysis. RISE installed the material on the MSU campus for field testing, and MSUToday covered the pilot demonstration.

Ni received a Best Poster award at MSU’s Graduate Research Symposium during his doctoral studies. Venkatesh Kodur, Weiyi Lu and Robert Ferrier served on his dissertation committee.
